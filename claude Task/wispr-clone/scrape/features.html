Refine

Flow edits while you speak.

Flow goes beyond basic dictation: cleaning up filler words, formatting lists, catching punctuation, and understanding corrections in real time.

Backtrack

Flow understands when you change your mind. Say “Let’s meet at 2… actually 3,” and Flow writes the corrected version instantly.

Remove fillers

Flow automatically removes “um,” “uh,” and other pauses so your text is clean and natural.

Numbered lists

Just speak the numbers: “Going to the store for 1. Apples 2. Bananas 3. Oranges.” Flow turns it into a formatted list.

Auto punctuation

Flow detects punctuation naturally from your pauses and tone. You can also dictate marks like “comma” or “question mark” for precision.

Build

Built for developers, not just dictation.

Flow understands syntax, file names, and code formatting so your voice fits seamlessly into your workflow.

Learn more
File tagging

If you’re using Cursor or Windsurf, Flow recognizes filenames as you speak and automatically tags the right file in your workspace, bringing all its context into your prompt. That means more precise AI responses, cleaner code, and less manual setup.

Syntax awareness

Dictation used to stumble on variables, so we fixed it. Flow parses your speech with full syntax awareness, ensuring code stays code. It interprets CLI commands, preserves exact spacing and formatting, and handles camelCase, snake_case, acronyms, and more.

Dev jargon

Flow automatically recognizes developer-specific terminology like Supabase, Cloudflare, and Vercel, keeping your dictation accurate in technical contexts. And for anything it doesn’t know yet, you can add it to your personal dictionary with a single correction.
