Meet Kitsu

A chibi fox that lives in the corner. Move your mouse and its eyes follow you. Hover to make it perk up, and click it a few times: there are three different reactions.

The buttons in the bottom-right corner sit behind the fox. Click the parts not covered by its body to check that the widget's invisible box doesn't block your page.

To use it on your site, copy everything between PET WIDGET START and PET WIDGET END into your page, just before </body>.
